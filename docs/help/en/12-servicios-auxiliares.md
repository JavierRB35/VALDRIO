# Contextual help: Auxiliary services report
**Last updated:** `2026-11-03`

## 1. Purpose and financial function
Read **Results and traceability → Auxiliary services**. This is no longer an independent screen. The report has no data entry, save, calculate or reopen actions. It preserves ThroughFlow and OwnerOnceCapacity policies and frozen economic evidence.

## 2. Fields and inputs
| Data | Source | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Auxiliary and unit | Published model | Physical magnitude; not every unit is an hour. |
| Normal and actual | Confirmed activity | Current physical data, or the frozen assessment for OwnerOnceCapacity. |
| Distribution | Compatible physical driver or saved measured service | No repeated entry. |

## 3. Results and interpretation
| Result | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Distributed | Actual activity times destination shares where a physical basis is identified, or saved measured services. |
| Unassigned | Actual minus distributed; unknown values remain unknown. |
| Unused capacity | Maximum of normal minus actual and zero; distinct from unassigned performed activity. |
| Detail | Destinations and the reason a value cannot be inferred. |

## 4. Use cases, validation and common errors
Historical percentage keys do not record their physical meaning. Do not convert such percentages into hours automatically. Equal physical distributions in multiple economic groups count once; conflicting groups are not added or selected arbitrarily. Consultation never reopens allocations or changes their economic policy. Reciprocal gross circulation is not an additional final cost.

## Recommended workflow

1. Complete the secondary allocation successfully; its result is saved automatically.
2. Open Allocation results and traceability and select the Auxiliary services report.
3. Compare normal, actual, distributed and unassigned activity, and inspect unused capacity.
4. Read the explanations when a compatible physical basis is missing. The report does not require entering services or reopening allocations.
